## Interpretation

The 2024 sentence already identifies coverage and fraud exposure. The two 2025 sentences add explicit cost and regulatory uncertainty. Both 2025 sentences are absent verbatim from the extracted 2024 Item 1A; that does not prove the underlying concepts were entirely new. More mentions measure disclosure emphasis, not successful adoption or increased realized losses.
